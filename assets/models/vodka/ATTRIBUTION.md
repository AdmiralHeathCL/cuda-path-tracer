The included Blender export has adjusted OBJ/MTL file references and relative
texture paths, plus an explicit white diffuse tint.

The renderer uses the base-color map with a diffuse material. The original normal,
roughness, metallic, AO and opacity images remain included, but these maps are not
applied by the current renderer. The README views are rendered adaptations of this
model with the project's preview lighting and floor. They are also distributed
under CC BY 4.0. No endorsement by the model's author is implied.
